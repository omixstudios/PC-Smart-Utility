❌ QR codes cannot contain files

        

          A QR code stores raw data — not a file on disk.

          The entire content must be encoded inside the image itself.

          Maximum QR capacity: ~2,900 bytes (about 2.8 KB)
        

      


      
      
Why every document is too big — even empty ones:

      

        

          FormatMin sizeFits in QR?
        

        

          📄 Word (.docx)~5–7 KB❌ Never
        

        

          📑 PDF~3–10 KB❌ Never
        

        

          📊 Excel (.xlsx)~4–6 KB❌ Never
        

        

          📷 Image (JPG/PNG)50 KB+❌ Never
        

        

          📦 ZIP / compressedstill KB+❌ Never
        

        

          📋 Plain text (.txt)< 1.8 KB✓ Only if tiny
        

      


      
      

        ⚠️ Compression doesn't help. QR codes already compress data internally. Even zipping a Word doc still leaves it at 4–6 KB — twice the QR limit. The phone scanner cannot decompress files from inside a QR.
      


      
      

        
✅ The correct way to share any document via QR:

        

          
1Upload your PDF / Word / Excel / image to Google Drive, OneDrive, or Dropbox

          
2Click Share → Copy Link (set to “Anyone with link can view”)

          
3Switch to the 🔗 Link tab here and paste that URL

          
4Generate → anyone who scans the QR opens your document instantly!
